Publications

Visual-Anchored Context Fusion for Source-Faithful Evidence Retrieval in Multimodal Fact Verification

Omid Tarabavar, Saeedeh Momtazi, and Reza Safabakhsh

Under review Applied Soft Computing

A training-free retrieval framework for multimodal fact verification. A visual-source ranking selects a protected set of evidence sources, contextual retrieval fills the remaining slots, and source-faithful restoration expands text only from the same page, so every piece of evidence keeps its provenance. Evaluated on AVerImaTeC.

Selected projects

Persian banking assistant: hybrid intent routing, RAG, and LLM-as-a-judge

Advanced NLP, 2026

A multi-turn chatbot where a fine-tuned ParsBERT classifier hands uncertain queries to a few-shot LLM, with LLM slot filling and hybrid retrieval with reranking. Intent accuracy rose from 87.7% to 96.2%; RAGAS and a custom judge agreed on correctness (r = 0.74) but barely on faithfulness (r = 0.19).

LoRA vs. hand-built adapters on Gemma 3n

Advanced NLP, 2026

Fine-tuned Gemma 3n on Persian news with LoRA and with bottleneck adapters written from scratch. With 64% fewer trainable parameters, the adapters reached lower in-domain perplexity and forgot less out of domain (+19.9% vs. +33.6%).

Adversarial robustness and fairness audit

Trustworthy ML, 2026

Attacks and adversarial training on Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10, checked for gradient masking with EOT-PGD, transfer attacks, and AutoAttack, plus a fairness, SHAP, and mitigation audit on Adult Income. PGD training lifted CIFAR-10 AutoAttack accuracy from 0% to 30.7%.

Code

Generalization under distribution shift

Trustworthy ML, 2026

Reweighting, DANN, and CycleGAN for domain adaptation on rotated MNIST: all recover part of a 15° shift and all fail at 90°. Transfer from 200 labeled samples reached 96.1%, against 80.2% from scratch.
